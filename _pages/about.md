Hello! I am Qiumeng Li, a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the University of Massachusetts Amherst.

Before moving to the U.S., I studied at China Agricultural University, where I completed both my bachelor’s and master’s degrees. During my master’s, I focused on harmonic analysis—a field that deepened my appreciation for the beauty of abstract mathematics.

Over time, my interests have shifted toward more applied directions, and now I focus on biomathematics, exploring how mathematical tools can help us understand complex biological systems and connect theory with real-world applications.
